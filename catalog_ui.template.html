About this catalog and its metadata

Search works across all fields, including titles, authors, tags, and notes. Multiple words must all match. Arabic/Persian letter variants and Latin accents are normalized for searching. Filters combine with the search. The default catalog order preserves the original category → subcategory → author → series/volume → title order.

Edition years are shown as recorded. “SH” means Solar Hijri; numerical year sorting does not convert between calendars. “Unknown” means missing or unverified information. Export CSV includes every matching result across all pages and all original columns. In Excel, import as UTF-8 with comma separators and set ISBN and Original ID to Text.
